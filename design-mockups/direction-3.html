Direction 3 — Data-Forward Minimal

Inspired by Bloomberg Terminal, Robinhood Pro, and Notion tables. This direction treats the app as a financial instrument, not a lifestyle product — near-black canvas, JetBrains Mono for all numbers (tabular, precise, machine-readable), hairline 1px borders as the only decoration. Color is used with extreme discipline: green only for on-track/positive delta, red only for over-budget, everything else is greyscale. Charts are the hero — they live full-bleed and take the most space. The UI gets out of your way and lets the data speak. Feels like you built it yourself.
